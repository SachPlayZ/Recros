How these recommendations are ranked

The report combines user–item similarity (0.30), similar-user affinity (0.20), genre (0.15), novelty (0.10), diversity (0.10), exposure balancing (0.10) and recency (0.05). Missing signals are omitted and available weights normalized.

Text and audio metadata work without user history. Historical movie ratings add audience affinity where available; there are no shared book/song users. The recent-release shelf is a small verified update. Exposure means rendered recommendation opportunities, not enjoyment or consumption.
