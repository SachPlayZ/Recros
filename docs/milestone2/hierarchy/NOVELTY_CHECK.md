# Attribution and targeted novelty check

October 14, 2026. This check accompanies a project-derived formulation; it does
not certify worldwide priority. Primary author/publisher sources were used for
technical comparisons; generic and generated web summaries were excluded.

Searches combined minimax regret, uncertain attributes/weights, hierarchies,
implications, maximum closure, minimum cuts, ontology faults and Lagrangian
bounds. They found strong component precedents and related elicitation. They
did not establish an identical signed-box/partial-label/implication formulation
with a structural-fault-budget extension. Absence of a keyword match cannot
establish originality; citation tracing and broader review remain necessary.

The precise candidate claim is: **an explicit tractable joint-uncertainty
formulation for incomplete hierarchical facets and one shared interval-box
preference vector; a conservative graph-computable structural-fault extension;
and a film/novel masking study quantifying phantom regret.**

Proposition 1 builds on graph-cut theory; Proposition 2 supplies the phantom-gap
construction; Proposition 3 applies Lagrangian bounding to this model. Proofs and
code are checkable regardless of the eventual originality assessment. Validity,
conditional usefulness and global originality remain separate claims.
